# Phase 6: Failure / Recovery Economics

We implemented a complete economic accounting for a scenario with 500 tasks, allowing up to 1 retry upon failure. 

### Economic Decomposition
- **Execution Cost:** Payments made to nodes for successful execution.
- **Failure Cost:** Payments wasted on failed executions (only applicable to non-contingent mechanisms).
- **Retry Cost:** Payments made to fallback nodes for executing retries.
- **SLA Loss:** The permanent economic penalty ($0.5 \times V_j$) incurred if a task fails all its retry attempts.
- **Total Economic Cost (Buyer):** `Execution Cost + Failure Cost + Retry Cost + SLA Loss`
- **Global Welfare (System):** $\sum (V_j - TrueCosts) - SLALoss$

### Results

| Mechanism | Successes | Retries | Exec Cost | Fail Cost | Retry Cost | SLA Loss | Total Econ Cost (Buyer) | Global Welfare |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `greedy` | 493.0 | 62.6 | $5,107 | $727 | $1,062 | $2,545 | **$9,441** | **229,251** |
| `edgetruth` | 496.3 | 29.3 | $11,647 | $0 | $943 | $511 | **$13,102** | **230,963** |

### Analysis: Is EdgeTruth creating greater economic value?

**1. System Perspective (Yes):**
EdgeTruth strictly creates **+$1,712** in greater global economic value (Welfare). It achieves this by routing tasks to highly reliable nodes, massively cutting down retries (from 62.6 to 29.3) and slashing permanent SLA losses by 80% (from $2,545 to $511).

**2. Buyer Perspective (Limitation Documented):**
Despite the system generating more value, the **Buyer** is economically worse off under EdgeTruth (Total Economic Cost rises from $9,441 to $13,102). 
- The buyer successfully avoids $727 in failure costs and $2,034 in SLA losses (Total saved: $2,761).
- However, to secure this reliability, the buyer pays a massive premium in Execution Costs (an extra $6,540).

**The Principled Limitation:**
Because `edgetruth` evaluates bids using the unified score $EU_i = P_i(V_j - Price_i) - (1-P_i)L_F$ but pays the node their first-price bid $Price_i$, highly reliable nodes possess immense market power. They realize they can inflate their $Price_i$ significantly above their true costs while still maintaining a higher $EU_i$ score than cheap/unreliable nodes. Consequently, the edge nodes extract almost all the surplus generated by the increased reliability, leaving the buyer with a net negative utility relative to greedy allocation. Unless a complex multidimensional VCG pricing rule is introduced (which we previously found can violate Individual Rationality), this surplus extraction by reliable nodes is a fundamental limitation of the market geometry.
